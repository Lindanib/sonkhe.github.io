Is Sonkhe available yet?

Sonkhe ERP is currently in development. This website introduces the product direction. Release dates, final features and pricing will be shared when confirmed.

Can we host it ourselves?

Yes. Sonkhe is being designed for a separate customer installation and database, hosted in your own environment or with your chosen hosting provider.

Do we have to purchase IT services?

No. Sonkhe implementation and product support stay with Sonkhe. PSU's optional infrastructure services are separately scoped and contracted.

Who is behind Sonkhe?

Sonkhe ERP is a product of Omnia Systems, an umbrella business for software products and professional IT services.
